Highwire Press / Google Scholar `citation_*` meta tags so the paper gets indexed by Google Scholar (Scholar's crawler looks for these specifically; OG/JSON-LD is not enough). Ref: https://scholar.google.com/intl/en/scholar/inclusion.html#indexing 2. Schema.org JSON-LD `ScholarlyArticle` for general crawlers, with authors, venue, date, identifiers, and code/PDF links.
